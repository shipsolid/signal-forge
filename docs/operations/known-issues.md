## Known issues

Recurring limitations and accepted trade-offs, consolidated from notes scattered across
[[reliability|reliability.md]], [[supply-chain|supply-chain.md]], [[testing|testing.md]], and
[[projects/app-signal-forge/operations/resilience-patterns|resilience-patterns.md]] "what this
doesn't cover" sections, plus code-level findings from writing those pages. This is not an incident
log — this is a personal lab with no on-call and no incident process (see
[README.md § Ownership Boundary](https://github.com/shipsolid/signal-forge/blob/main/README.md#ownership-boundary))
— it is a single place to check before assuming a gap is new.

Planned/deferred work that's already tracked with a target is in
[README.md § Roadmap](https://github.com/shipsolid/signal-forge/blob/main/README.md#roadmap)
(a live external SLO/telemetry promotion gate, Pyroscope profiling, Faro source-map upload, datastore
HA operator migration) and is not duplicated here.
